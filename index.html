About Me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Learning Sciences and Psychological Studies at UNC Chapel Hill. I study how students develop self-regulated learning skills and how to support that development with AI. I also expect to complete a Master of Applied Data Science in December 2026.

I design and test theory-informed AI interventions, develop LLM methods to code learners' spoken descriptions of their thinking, and develop causal methods to test who benefits from an intervention. In my dissertation, I compare adaptive feedback through SoL2LBot with multimedia strategy instruction to study how students learn to plan their work.

Outside research, I love spending time with my daughter. I also enjoy K-pop dance and listening to jazz and funk.
