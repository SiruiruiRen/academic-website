Research Projects

LLM-Based Coding of SRL Think-Aloud Protocols

I used an established self-regulated learning codebook to design and evaluate LLM coding prompts. By comparing model classifications with human coding in mathematics and biology tasks, I identified when coding accuracy depends on the learning process, task, and prompting approach.

Published · Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(2), 1–24 (2026)

LLM Feedback for Teacher Professional Vision

With colleagues in the INFER project, I compared prompting approaches and studied how pre-service teachers used AI feedback to revise classroom-video analyses. In a follow-up study, I examined in-service teachers' feedback uptake across successive drafts.

Published · LAK26 (2026)
Follow-up manuscript under review · Learning and Instruction

SoL2LBot: Adaptive Support for Study Planning

I led the intervention design with my research team and independently built the SoL2LBot platform. In my dissertation trial, I compare adaptive AI feedback with multimedia strategy instruction and test whether students apply their planning skills in later tasks.

Dissertation research · University of Nevada, Las Vegas

Causal Moderation with Continuous Moderators

I developed a double-weighting estimator for standardized causal moderation in observational studies. I use this approach to adjust for measured background differences when estimating how treatment effects vary across a continuous moderator, under stated assumptions about confounding.

Under review · Multivariate Behavioral Research

Digital Self-Regulation Interventions in STEM

I study how students use self-regulated learning strategies after digital training in STEM courses. In related collaborative work, I examine learning sequences in course logs and associations between students' motivation, early performance, and dropout.

Center for Learning Analytics, UNC Chapel Hill

Resource-Intervention Match Framework

I introduced the Resource-Intervention Match framework to explain why students may respond differently to the same guidance. I focus on how intervention design matches students' prior knowledge, self-regulated learning skills, and motivational resources.

Published · Learning and Individual Differences (2026)
