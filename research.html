Research Focus

Self-Regulated Learning

What guidance do students need, and when can they use a learning strategy independently?

I introduced the Resource-Intervention Match framework in Learning and Individual Differences (2026) to explain how students' knowledge, skills, and motivational resources may affect their responses to guidance. I apply this framework in SoL2LBot, an AI-based study-planning scaffold, and compare adaptive feedback with multimedia strategy instruction in my dissertation trial.

LLMs in Education

When can researchers rely on LLM coding to study learning processes?

In my Journal of Learning Analytics article (2026), I evaluated LLM coding of think-aloud data against human coding across tasks, learning processes, and prompting approaches. I also study how teachers use AI feedback to revise classroom-video analyses, with a published LAK26 paper on pre-service teachers and a follow-up manuscript on in-service teachers under review at Learning and Instruction.

Causal Inference Methods

How do intervention effects vary across learner characteristics?

I develop causal moderation methods to estimate these differences. In a manuscript under review at Multivariate Behavioral Research, I developed a double-weighting estimator to adjust for measured background differences in observational studies. I also co-authored a practical guide to causal moderation analysis in the Journal of School Psychology (2025).

Learning Analytics

How do students' digital actions relate to their learning processes?

I combine think-aloud data and digital traces to examine which actions accompany specific self-regulated learning processes. My collaborative work includes interpreting learning sequences in a college mathematics course and examining associations between motivation, early course performance, and STEM dropout.
